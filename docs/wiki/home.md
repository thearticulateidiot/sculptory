# Sculptory wiki

A world editor for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric). Press `B` to open it, `Ctrl+K` to find any command by name, `F1` for every key. Pick a page by what you want to do. Every page ends with related pages, and the **Home** button above brings you back here.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install it and make a first edit
- [Tutorial](tutorial.md): short lessons in your own world
- [Editor mode](editor-mode.md): open, look around, fly, leave
- [The screen and windows](screen-and-windows.md): what is where, and how to arrange it
- [Finding things](finding-things.md): menus, `Ctrl+K`, `F1`, this wiki

## Select and change blocks

- [Select](select.md): a box, a shape, a brush or a lasso
- [Selection operations](selection-operations.md): Fill, Replace (Keep shape, Whole family), Erase, Hollow, Walls, Overlay, Naturalize, Update blocks
- [Tinker](tinker.md): change one block's properties, a sign's text or an entity in place
- [History](history.md): undo, redo, the History window

## Copy, paste and share

- [Clipboard](clipboard.md): copy and cut
- [Place](place.md): paste, move and stack, turn and flip
- [Library](library.md): the server's shared schematics and palettes
- [Import and export](import-export.md): `.schem`, `.litematic` and `.nbt` files

## Shape the land

- [Terrain brushes](terrain-brushes.md): Raise, Lower, Smooth, Flatten, Paint
- [Masks](masks.md): limit every edit, or one brush, to the blocks you choose
- [Weather brush](weather.md): erode, fill in, roughen or melt terrain
- [Shape brush](shape-brush.md): spheres, cylinders, cones, cubes, pyramids
- [Extrude](extrude.md): pull a face out, carve it in, smear it along
- [Fluid](fluid.md): flood, drain, balls of water or lava
