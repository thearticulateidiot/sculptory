# The Sculptory name and logo

The code is free software (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md`): you may copy it, change it and share your changes. The
name is different. "Sculptory" and the Sculptory logo tell players that what they download is the project's own
build. This page says, in plain words, how you may use them. It is the policy that section 3 of the additional terms
in `NOTICE.md` refers to.

## You may, without asking

- **Share unmodified official builds under the name**: in modpacks, on mirrors, on your server's download page, or
  bundled with an installer, as long as the files are exactly as released and you don't present yourself as the
  project.
- **Say what your work uses or works with**: "uses Sculptory", "for Sculptory", "compatible with Sculptory", "an
  addon for Sculptory". An addon may have "for Sculptory" in its name, but its own name must come first and it must
  not look official. (Addons that use Sculptory's code or interfaces must also be open source under an
  AGPL-compatible licence; see `docs/LICENSING.md`.)
- **Make content about it**: videos, streams, tutorials, reviews, screenshots, guides, wiki pages, fan art, server
  listings that say the server runs Sculptory. Showing the logo to point at the project is fine.
- **Say where a fork comes from**: "based on Sculptory", "a fork of Sculptory".

## You may not

- **Publish or sell a modified version, fork or rebrand under the Sculptory name or logo**, or under a name or logo
  that could be mistaken for them (such as "Sculptory Plus" or "Sculptory 2"). Give it a name of its own, and say it
  is based on Sculptory. Internal ids (the mod id, namespaces and channel ids) may stay the same where you need them
  for compatibility.
- **Suggest that the project endorses, makes or supports** your build, server, product or service when it doesn't.
- **Use the name in your own product, company or domain name** in a way that suggests it is the project.

## Good to know

- Modified versions running on your own server don't need a new name, but you must not tell players it is the
  official build. The AGPL asks you to offer them its source (see `docs/LICENSING.md`).
- This page covers the name, the icon and the logo (all rights reserved, see `NOTICE.md`). What you may do with the code and the docs is set by their licences.
- If you're unsure, or want to do something this page doesn't allow, open an issue and ask.
